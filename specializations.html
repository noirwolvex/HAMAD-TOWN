HOW TO CHOOSE

Make your choice with confidence.

A good decision starts with self-understanding, then checking the details.

01

Start with your interests

Which lessons make you curious? Which activities do you enjoy exploring after class?

02

Recognise your strengths

Think about the subjects where you learn confidently and the skills you want to improve.

03

Check future requirements

Review the admission conditions for the university programmes that interest you.

04

Ask for guidance

Discuss your options with your family, teachers and school guidance counsellor.

IMPORTANT ADMISSION NOTE

Your track is a starting point, not a university admission guarantee.

University admission requirements vary by programme. They may include a required school track, specific subjects, a minimum average, an admission test or an interview. Always check the latest requirements before choosing your subjects.
